#OpChildSafety

Privacy-Safe Child Abuse Reporting Directory

⚠️ If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or police immediately.

About This Site

This site is a static directory of official child abuse reporting resources.
It does not collect data, run scripts, log visitors, or store reports.
All links redirect you to official reporting authorities.

Safety & OPSEC Tips

  • Use private/incognito browsing
  • Avoid shared or monitored devices
  • Clear browser history after reporting
  • Consider Tor Browser if at risk
  • Never download or store illegal material
This site is static, open-source, and stores no user data.
